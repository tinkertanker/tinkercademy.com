<ArticleLegacyBlock as="h2" html={"How does Tank Tinkerer work?"} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"We (well… mostly Jerrayl) made the game in Unity, out of a variety of open-source models and prefabs—you can grab a copy of the source code here:"} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="aside" html={"<a href=\"http://github.com/Tinkertanker/tanktinkerer\"><strong>Tinkertanker/TankTinkerer</strong></a><a href=\"http://github.com/Tinkertanker/tanktinkerer\"><br /></a><a href=\"http://github.com/Tinkertanker/tanktinkerer\"><em>TankTinkerer Game</em></a><a href=\"http://github.com/Tinkertanker/tanktinkerer\">github.com</a>"} className="article-link-card" />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"<em><strong>Aside:</strong></em><em> Look at that URL! Tinkertanker/TankTinkerer! Ahha haaha haha! Oh, ahem, sorry.</em>"} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"<strong>How do controllers talk to the game?</strong> The Tank Tinkerer game reads serial input from a <em>receiver </em>micro:bit, connected to the game computer by USB. The micro:bit controllers, or <em>senders</em>, simply send keyboard commands (e.g. W to move forward, S to move back) to the received to control the game. Our <a href=\"https://docs.google.com/document/d/1cwugjOTJTUyKDmO4TNX1o4s0DKTSO2RnFVEE9vkV93M/\">installation guide</a> has information anyone needs to get the game set up properly."} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"<strong>Won’t participants experience lots of lag time by having to send their controls over the micro:bit radio? </strong>Yes. This is intentional, and also hilarious to watch."} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"<strong>How do participants win? </strong>We split the competition into two main categories, with prizes for the top controller designs, and a 64-team tournament for the best players. We’re also hoping to see some teams dress up for the Best Theme."} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="h2" html={"Why this competition?"} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"When designing this competition, we were heavily inspired by the Nintendo LABO launch video from earlier this year:"} />

<ArticleEmbed provider="youtube" href={"https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P3Bd3HUMkyU"} title={"First Look at Nintendo Labo"} description={"Get ready to Make, Play and Discover with Nintendo Labo! Nintendo Labo is a new line of interactive build-and-play experiences that combine DIY creations wit..."} captionHtml={""} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"By having the competition be about designing game controllers, we aim to have participants create physical, real-world objects that interact with our game’s virtual world. We think this is a great way to bring together maker concepts such as sensors &amp; actuators, inter-device connectivity, and even a dash of augmented reality, to excite participants into thinking up their next great connected device project."} />
