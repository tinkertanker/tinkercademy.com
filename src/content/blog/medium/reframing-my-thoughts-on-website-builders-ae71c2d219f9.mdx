<ArticleLegacyBlock as="h2" html={"How Does ‘Framer’ Work?"} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"Now I know you’ve heard this already; multiple times. You simply drag-and-drop the components you want into Framer to build your website, just like you would on design platforms such as Figma or Sketch. Framer is as simple as it gets."} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"Web development on Framer is a piece of cake and if you’re familiar with Figma, that’s a bonus because Framer shares a very similar layout and workflow to Figma, making it that much more easier to grasp."} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"Whilst Framer is intuitive and user-friendly, it has many functionalities which can overload beginner developers. Hence, make sure to start with the basic and gain a strong grasp of them before leaping into advanced animations, interactivity, dynamism, etc."} />
