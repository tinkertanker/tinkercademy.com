<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"<a href=\"http://makerfairesingapore.com\">Maker Faire Singapore</a>’s 6th iteration was held at the Singapore Science Centre, and we decided to one-up the 3 escape rooms we made for 2016, and build a cardboard arcade (inspired by <a href=\"http://cainesarcade.com/\">Caine Monroy</a>—yes, 5 years late, but inspiration never grows old!)."} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="blockquote" html={"<em>“I thought we said last year never to do this again”</em>"} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"In 2 months, we churned out 6 different games, a life-size dismantle-able Tardis, an arcade credit system, and chugged through too much cardboard and coffee to count. Weekends and weeknights were burned and ever so often you’d hear someone lament ‘<em>I thought we said last year never to do this again</em>’."} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"However, making for Maker Faire (or any event for that matter) has a mysterious pull—you come up with a crazy idea, start working on the crazy idea, sign yourself up for a showcase deadline, and realise that your crazy idea will never be done in time for the deadline… but you’re an arm and a leg deep in it already and hey, sunk cost! So you keep sinking more in."} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"But when it’s all over, you rise to the surface again, or get washed up to shore, (whatever floats your analogous boat <code>;)</code>), and realise that you’re still alive. Rosy retrospection kicks in, and you finally are able to talk about your experience. Maybe it takes a month, but here we are, and here’s what we did for Maker Faire SG 2017: <strong>Tinkerbot’s Arcade of Many Bits</strong>."} />

<ArticleLegacyBlock as="p" html={"(We tried calling it <strong>Tinkerbot&#39;s Arcade of Many Bits Over Up Rolling In Nice Exciting Stuff</strong>, for <strong>TAMBOURINES</strong>, but that… was a bit much.)"} />
