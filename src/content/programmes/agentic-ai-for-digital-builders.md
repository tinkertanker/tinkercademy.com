## Course Overview

<p dir="auto">You've vibe coded your first prototypes. The next step is not another app, it's automating the work itself. This hands-on sequel to <em>Vibe Coding for Digital Builders</em> introduces <strong>Agentic AI</strong>: systems that plan, use tools, and complete long-horizon tasks with minimal supervision.</p><p dir="auto">We'll ground the theory in <strong>OpenClaw</strong>, the open-source agent framework that popularised the <em>Plan → Act → Observe → Re-plan</em> loop, and then put it into practice on <strong>Claude Cowork</strong>, Anthropic's managed agent platform. Across the session you'll build four production-shaped agents that mirror the recurring work knowledge teams do every week — from statistics scraping to daily news briefings.</p>

## Lesson Outcomes

<p dir="auto">By the end of this course, you will have designed, deployed and demonstrated agents on Claude Cowork that can:</p><ul dir="auto"><li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Scrape and reshape public data.</strong> Pull weekly figures from public statistical sources, clean the output against your company's data schema, and generate on-brand visualisations and insights with no manual intervention. <em>(We'll supply the sample sources and target schema.)</em></p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Turn source documents into presentations.</strong> Extract content from long-form reports and shared document stores, synthesise the findings, and render a narrative-driven slide deck into your company's presentation template. <em>(We'll supply a sample report and a template to populate.)</em></p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Close the loop on budget tracking.</strong> Ingest spend data exported from a finance system, populate a budget tracker, and deliver a visualised budget report a director can read at a glance. <em>(We'll supply dummy spend data and a tracker workbook.)</em></p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Run a daily intelligence desk.</strong> Monitor global news and public sentiment in your sector, rank issues by relevance to your organisation's priorities, and produce cited daily briefings plus a weekly roundup — reproducing a reference pair of reports end-to-end. <em>(We'll supply the reference reports.)</em></p></li></ul><p dir="auto">Along the way you'll pick up the underlying agentic patterns — tool use, structured outputs, long-running tasks, human-in-the-loop review — that transfer to whatever agent platform your team standardises on next.</p>
