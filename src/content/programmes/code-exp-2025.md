## Course Overview

<p>CODE_EXP is a <strong>mobile dev hackathon </strong>spread over three phases:</p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Phase 1: <strong>Training sessions</strong></p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Phase 2: <strong>Qualifiers</strong></p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Phase 3: <strong>Finals</strong></p></li></ul>

## Lesson Outcomes

<ol><li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Gain Hands-on Experience with React Native</strong></p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Understand the fundamentals of React Native for cross-platform mobile development.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Build functional mobile UI components and integrate backend services.</p></li></ul></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Master the End-to-End Prototyping Process</strong></p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Learn how to ideate, wireframe, and prototype a mobile app concept effectively.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Translate user needs into intuitive app designs.</p></li></ul></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Integrate Generative AI into Mobile Apps</strong></p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Explore how to leverage generative AI tools (e.g. LLMs) to enhance app functionality.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Understand practical use cases and best practices for AI integration.</p></li></ul></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Work with Edge LLMs</strong></p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Learn about deploying LLMs on-device or on the edge for faster, privacy-preserving AI features.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Understand the trade-offs and constraints of edge computing in mobile environments.</p></li></ul></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Develop Communication &amp; Pitching Skills</strong></p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Craft a compelling 1-minute elevator pitch video.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Create a persuasive slide deck that clearly articulates the value proposition and architecture of your app.</p></li></ul></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Learn Agile, Time-Conscious Product Development</strong></p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Practice working within tight deadlines to ideate, develop, and iterate on a mobile app concept.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Experience what it's like to build MVPs in a fast-paced, real-world scenario.</p></li></ul></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Compete and Collaborate Under Pressure</strong></p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Improve teamwork and decision-making in a live, competitive setting.</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Receive feedback from judges and peers to iterate on your project.</p></li></ul></li></ol><p><br></p>
