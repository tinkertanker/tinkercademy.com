## Course Overview

<p>UI/UX design and Figma prototyping for primary, secondary, and junior college students. Students begin with user needs, personas, user flows, and wireframes, then design app screens, apply visual-system principles, and build clickable prototypes tested with real users.</p>
<p>These are the same research, design, and prototyping practices used by product teams in industry, and the skills involved — understanding users, organising ideas, communicating visually, and improving work through feedback — carry into computing, design, and project work at every level. No design or coding experience is needed.</p>
<p>The course is offered at two levels, listed separately on the IMDA LEARN Roadmaps application. Projects scale with level: older students take on more complex briefs, with greater emphasis on clarity and user fit:</p>
<ul>
	<li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Primary 4–6:</strong> an introduction to design thinking through FigJam and Figma — students turn a school, home, or community problem into a persona, screen flow, and clickable prototype.</p></li>
	<li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Secondary 1–5, JC 1–2:</strong> industry-grade UI/UX plus a guided introduction to AI-enabled prototyping with Figma Make — students practise a prompt-and-evaluate cycle, comparing AI-generated outputs against their own intentions rather than accepting them wholesale.</p></li>
</ul>

## Lesson Outcomes

<ul>
	<li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Frame digital product challenges around users, personas, needs, and constraints.</p></li>
	<li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Create user journeys, low-fidelity wireframes, and screen flows before visual design.</p></li>
	<li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Use FigJam to organise ideas and Figma to design structured app screens and reusable interface patterns.</p></li>
	<li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Apply visual-system principles including colour, typography, alignment, spacing, components, and interface states.</p></li>
	<li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Build and test clickable Figma prototypes with real users, and present design decisions to an audience.</p></li>
	<li data-preset-tag="p"><p>At sec/JC level: evaluate AI-generated design outputs critically using a guided prompt-and-evaluate cycle.</p></li>
</ul>

## Details

<p><strong>Project</strong></p>
<p>Each student creates and presents a clickable app prototype for a real user need — a portfolio piece for school showcases and competitions.</p>
<p><strong>Requirements</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Computer:</strong> A laptop, desktop, Chromebook, or school PLD running Windows 8.1+, macOS 11+, or a recent ChromeOS or Linux — 64-bit, with WebGL enabled and a stable internet connection. 8 GB RAM recommended. iPads are not suitable for screen design.</p></li>
	<li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Software:</strong> Figma Design and FigJam on the free Figma Education plan, in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. Nothing to install; our trainer sets up student accounts with the school in the first lesson. Figma Make is used in one guided lesson at sec/JC level, with access provided by Tinkertanker at no extra cost. School IT should allow access to figma.com. The primary-level course uses no AI tools.</p></li>
	<li data-preset-tag="p"><p><strong>Hardware:</strong> None required.</p></li>
</ul>
