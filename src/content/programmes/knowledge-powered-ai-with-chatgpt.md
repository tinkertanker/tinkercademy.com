## Course Overview

<p>Generative AI isn't just transforming how we create content. It's revolutionising how organisations <em>access</em> and <em>leverage</em> their knowledge. Whether you're looking to enhance customer service, automate content creation, or develop intelligent knowledge systems, this workshop will equip you with the tools to harness AI's full potential within your organization.</p><p>In this course, available as half-, one, or two-day versions, we'll guide you through the cutting edge of generative AI technology. You'll start by understanding the digital landscape and exploring how large language models like ChatGPT function, before diving into practical applications of ChatGPT and other AI tools:</p><ul><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Understanding Large Language Models (LLMs), generative AI possibilities and limitations</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Prompt engineering</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Image generation with GPT-4o</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Canvas for collaborative writing and coding with AI</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Data analysis tools</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Projects</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Deep Research</p></li><li data-preset-tag="p"><p>Retrieval-Augmented Generation</p></li></ul><p>This hands-on, executive-focused workshop is perfect for senior leaders, innovation teams, and decision-makers.
